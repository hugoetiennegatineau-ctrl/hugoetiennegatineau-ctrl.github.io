❓ What is CoCo3_MCP_Server?

CoCo3_MCP_Server is a special helper program that lets your computer talk to a Color Computer 3 (also known as TRS-80) emulator. Think of it as a translator between your modern computer and this classic 1980s computer system.

This software uses the powerful MAME emulator engine to recreate the authentic experience of using a Tandy Color Computer 3. Whether you want to explore vintage software, play classic games, or learn about early personal computing, this server makes it easy and enjoyable.

Why use this? It takes all the complicated technical setup and makes it simple. Just download, run, and you're ready to explore the world of 8-bit computing!
